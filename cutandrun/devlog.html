Day 2 — 2026-11-13 (금)

기획 (15:00–16:30 KST)

  • 잼 주제 DIRECTOR'S CUT 확인. 공식 설명은 "묵혀 둔 아이디어를 만들고, AI를 감독하고, 무엇을 최종본에 넣을지 정하라".
  • 출발점: 어릴 때 하던 스타크래프트 유즈맵 '톰과제리' 같은 경찰과 도둑 게임.
  • 기획이 세 번 바뀌었다. 1. 감독이 도둑 크루를 RTS로 지휘하는 싱글 게임 2. 비슷한 게임 조사 → 메카 카멜레온 기준으로는 신박함이 부족하다는 판단 3. 최종: 멀티 전용 경찰과 도둑. 숨고, 잡고, 풀어주고. 덕몽어스식 임무 → 스킬업. 도둑은 동료가 잡혀갈수록 약해져서 구출하러 가야만 한다.
  • 결정: 솔로 개발, 봇 없음, 은신은 "흐릿하게 보일 뿐"이고 수색 발각 판정은 없음.

제작 (16:30– KST)

  • 원격 GPU PC(COM5080)에 UE 5.8 C++ 프로젝트를 만들고, 에셋 없이 코드로 세트를 짓는 구조를 택했다. 맵 레이아웃이 텍스트 한 파일(CnRLayout.h)이라 에이전트가 고치고 검증하기 쉽다.
  • 검증 루프: 테스트 전용 자동 조종(-CnRBot=auto)을 켠 게임 창 3개(호스트 1 + 클라이언트 2)를 띄워 한 판을 돌리고, 로그 이벤트와 스크린샷으로 확인한다. 자동 조종은 개발 빌드에만 들어가는 테스트 도구이고 출시 빌드에는 없다.
  • M1 결과: 첫 빌드부터 성공. 3인 네트워크에서 접속, 카운트다운, 임무, 체포, 감옥, 승패, 판마다 진영 교대가 작동했다.
  • M2: 경찰 손전등 시야, 밤 조명, 은신 구역, 달리기 소리, 레벨업 카드, 진영별 스킬(대시, 연막, 대역 판넬, 점프컷 / 질주, 수갑, 추적, 드론 등), 2인 금고, 경찰 임무.
  • 패키지 빌드(Development) 첫 시도 성공. 쿠킹된 빌드에서도 머티리얼과 캐릭터가 정상이고 네트워크 접속이 된다. GameFeature 설정 경고는 원인 설정을 지워 해결했다.
  • 메인 메뉴(이름, 방 만들기, IP 참가)를 에셋 없이 Slate로 만들었다.
  • 갇힌 도둑은 흑백 CCTV 전체 맵 뷰로 바뀌고, 클릭으로 팀에게 핑을 찍는다.
  • 판이 끝나면 체포, 탈옥, 점프컷, 대역 판넬, 탈출 순간의 스틸컷이 '디렉터스 컷' 필름 스트립으로 나온다.
  • 사운드: 외부 음원 대신 Python으로 효과음 18개와 케이퍼 재즈 루프 2개를 합성했다. 라이선스 문제가 없다.
  • Tripo로 촬영장 소품 6종(금고, 돈가방, 필름 카메라, 조명 스탠드, 의상 행거, 감독 의자)을 만들어 임무 지점, 은신 구역, 가로등, 세트 장식에 넣었다.
  • 도둑은 오브젝트 공간 줄무늬 머티리얼로 흑백 죄수복 + 비니, 경찰은 파란 제복 + 모자. 위에서 봐도 한눈에 구분된다.
  • 엔진이 접속할 때 플레이어 이름을 온라인 ID로 덮어쓰는 문제를 찾아, 접속 직후 메뉴 이름을 서버로 보내도록 고쳤다. 한글 이름도 HUD에 정상으로 나온다.
  • Shipping 빌드는 보안상 명령줄 맵 지정을 무시한다. 그래서 테스트와 바로가기용으로 -CnRHost / -CnRJoin=IP 옵션을 만들었다.
  • 미니맵 추가.

Day 2 (evening) — 2026-11-13: change of direction

The entries above describe the first, top-down version. They are kept as written, including the 5.8 and top-down details that no longer apply. The entries below were written in English and are in the order things happened. Times are not recorded for these.

  • First-person rewrite. The top-down prototype was rewritten as a first-person game. The top-down source is kept in Source_backup_topdown_20261113 and in git commit aa8e5e8.
  • New art direction. The American-style film set was replaced by a Joseon night village. Every player wears a paper name-tag sticker on the chest and back: "도둑" (thief) or "포졸" (constable) plus the player's name, drawn at runtime. The old Tripo props for the film, Western and spaceship sets became unused (about 180 Tripo credits spent on the second batch).
  • Rules simplified. The win rules now center on the jail. All thieves jailed means a constable win, and on time-out thieves win only if more are free than jailed. The exits and the progress gauge were removed.
  • Moved to UE 5.9. A commit before the switch is kept so the project can go back to 5.8.4. There was one GPU crash on 5.9.0 (DXGI_ERROR_DEVICE_HUNG) at the moment of going from the menu to hosting. It did not reproduce, and there was none in the 20 or more runs since. I do not know the cause.
  • First Codex review (of the top-down code): 10 issues, all fixed. It was written by a different model from the one that wrote the code. Examples: decoy positions could differ between clients, a stunned player could still finish a rescue, two rescue gates finishing together paid the reward twice, a thrown rope could pass through a thief on a slow server frame. The first-person change removed one issue by itself (a hidden enemy visible for one frame).
  • My own review found more. A decoy kept sliding after it stopped, Esc-to-leave depended on a console command that may fail in Shipping (now it needs two presses and uses the engine's path), music could overlap after leaving and re-entering, and a rescue held through a respawn was lost.
  • Real input test. Until now every test used the auto-pilot, so real keyboard and mouse input had never been verified. I sent real keys and mouse movement on the test PC. Movement worked (474 cm/s measured), but mouse look was far too fast. The look scale was reduced from 2.2 to 0.6.
  • Shipping build and multiplayer. A Shipping build was packaged and one host plus two clients joined each other on a single PC.
  • Removed the experimental AI toolset plugins. They broke the Shipping cook on 5.9, so they were taken out of the project.
  • Second Codex review (of the first-person code): 8 issues, all fixed. - The time-out result could be changed by a catch in the same frame. - A late interact message could act on the next take's pawn. - The aim point came from a stale camera pose. - A jailed thief watching from above could not hear the cell. - The "seen" check ignored looking up or down. - Faintly visible thieves showed as bright dots on the minimap. - The rope could skip thin cover. - Crosshair traces could pass through the player being aimed at (the eighth issue, from reviews\codex-review-2.md).
  • Post-process look. An ink-and-paper post-process was added: outlines drawn before anti-aliasing and smoke, then soft tone steps and hanji grain after tone mapping.
  • Art by Codex. A separate Codex worker generated the Joseon 2D set (key art, cover, icon, 12 skill icons, hanji texture). I reviewed the images and the Hangul on the tags stroke by stroke. Tripo made the Joseon village props.
  • Docs. The GDD was rewritten as v0.4 for the new direction. The itch page draft, playtest guide and asset list were rewritten to match.

Day 2 (late evening) — 2026-11-13: bots added, "no bots" reversed

The decision above ("solo development, no bots", and "multiplayer only" in the entries before it) is kept as written. It no longer holds.

  • Bots. Empty seats are now filled by server-side bots, so one person can host and play alone. The reason: judges may run the game by themselves, and a multiplayer-only build would show them only a menu. Default fill target is 4 players in total (for example 1 human + 3 bots). In the lobby the host presses B to cycle the fill: off / 4 / 5 / 6. With bots off, 3 humans are needed to start, as before. A human who joins takes a bot's seat from the next take. At most 6 players are active. Bots carry a small "BOT" stamp on their name tag in the HUD. Bots play both roles: hide, jobs, rescue, shoe throws, chase, searching hiding places, guarding the jail. Command line: CutAndRun.exe -CnRHost -CnRName=Solo -CnRBots=4.
  • Hide phase. Each take now starts with 20 seconds in which the constables' screen is black ("EYES SHUT" with a countdown) while the thieves scatter and hide ("HIDE!"). The take clock starts when the constables are released. This replaces the 5 second blind start.
  • Straw-shoe throw. Every free thief can throw a straw shoe with the right mouse button: 14 second cooldown, about 18 m range, a hit slows the constable by 45% for 2.5 seconds. No XP. It lets thieves help a friend who is being chased.
  • More hiding places. Fences, sheds, washing lines and straw sheaves were added to the jar yards. There are now 31 hiding places (counted from HideZones() in CnRLayout.h), up from 13.
  • Paper UI. The HUD and menu were restyled as paper name-tag slips in a hand-made variety-caption style: torn hanji paper, masking tape, red seal stamps. Fonts: Black Han Sans, Nanum Brush Script, Gaegu, Noto Sans (SIL OFL 1.1).
  • Docs. GDD, itch page, playtest guide, asset list and review record were updated for these changes.
  • Not verified yet. The server bots and the new rules had not been played with real input at this point. (Later the same night: see the next entry.)

Not verified yet (as of the late evening; the night entry below updates this)

  • Play across two PCs over a real network. Everything so far ran on one PC.
  • The audio by ear. It is all synthesized and nobody has listened to it.
  • Balance and fun with real players. Constable speed (12% faster) and the rescue and round lengths need friend testing. (The 3 second rescue and the 5 minute round were changed later that night.)

Day 2 (night) — 2026-11-13: real-input play test, judging round, fixes

Times are not recorded. The order is the order things happened.

  • Real-input play test. Real keyboard and mouse input was sent to the build at 2560x1440 full screen, driven by the agent on the test PC: the main menu, hosting alone, starting, movement, look, a skill, holding E at a job, throwing the shoe, being caught, the jail view and the results screen. All of it worked. The menu path that earlier only the command line had covered is now checked by hand. This corrects the older "clicking through the main menu has not been checked" note.
  • The first bot takes were too short. In the first automatic bot matches, with a 10 second hide phase, all three thieves were caught 25 to 35 seconds after the constable was released, in two takes out of three. The reason: bot thieves walked out to do jobs the moment the constable was released. In one real-input take as a thief, both bot teammates ran to the rescue gate one after the other and were caught there, and the take was lost in 45 seconds. After tuning, bots lie low first and a take lasted about 1 minute 55 seconds. The bots' pathing also did not know about trees, the well and stalls.
  • Judging round. Five judges scored the 22:00 build: three Claude subagents (gameplay, art and UX, theme and originality) and two Codex workers (technical, level and systems). They read the docs and source, looked at 14 real-play screenshots and the bot match numbers; none of them ran the game. The totals out of 80 were 43, 45, 42, 46 and 45, average 44.2 (table in REVIEW.md). Most common complaints: balance leaned to the constables (6 of 7 bot matches won by the constables in 51 to 143 seconds), nothing to do after being caught, a weak theme on screen, a HUD covering 27% of the screen, unreadable name tags, a 3 second rescue that was slower than a constable standing in the middle needs to reach either gate (about 2.3 seconds), bot pathing that ignored trees, the well and stalls, catches through thin fences, abuse of joining mid-take, a held E key that could drop, and a 9 second music loop with electronic stingers.
  • Rules changed. A take is now 3 minutes instead of 5. A tie at time-out goes to the thieves. Rescue is 2 seconds instead of 3. The three drum posts share one 35 second cooldown. Jump Cut unlocks at level 2 instead of 4. Constable XP from prisoners was halved. Thieves start spread left and right. A player who joins or rejoins mid-take watches and gets a seat from the next take. A catch needs a clear line between constable and thief (scarecrows too). A held E is re-sent regularly so it cannot drop. C asks for the constable role in the next take.
  • Bots reworked. The path grid is built from real collision, so trees, the well, stalls and lamp posts are avoided. Thief bots lie low after the constables open their eyes, wait a few seconds before a rescue (shorter when a person is jailed), send one rescuer at a time, and move to other cover when the drum marks them. They act on a jailed player's click: a ping on the jail means "rescue now" for the nearest bot, a ping elsewhere means "the constable is there". Constable bots see far only inside the lantern beam, pause after a catch, give up long chases and use the drum only after a long empty search.
  • Straw shoe. With a constable in plain sight within 14 m the shoe is now aimed at them automatically, whichever way the thief faces, at 20 m/s. Before the change the shoe hit 0 times in 7 takes.
  • Language. The game is Korean by default and can switch to English with a title-menu button or the L key, or -CnRLang=en / -CnRLang=ko.
  • Film framing on screen (the theme was the weakest score): a clapperboard slate on the countdown, a film-leader countdown with a short briefing on the constable's eyes-shut screen, letterbox bars in the hide phase, a freeze frame on CUT, screenplay scene headings when you enter a quarter, and a Director's Cut results screen with up to 6 stills, three pre-selected and the first shown as a poster with a film title, red seal rating, acting awards and "Directed by <you>". The jailed view became the director's monitor, with a REC stamp and timecode.
  • HUD and look. Only owned skill cards are shown, the role card is small and the controls are on F1. Name tags are larger in the game's own typeface, with a red 봇 seal for bots. Thieves wear hemp white with an ochre headband, constables indigo with a red sash and a wider hat. Lantern-style markers on poles replaced the light pillars, and the floating English quarter signs were removed.
  • Audio. Rebuilt with the same two Python scripts: a 20-bar piece (about 46 seconds) and a 16-bar fast variant (about 29 seconds) for gayageum, geomungo, janggu and gongs. The alarm, win stingers, clapper, action, level-up, mission, jailbreak and NG cues were re-voiced with the same instruments. Nobody has listened to it critically.
  • Font. Noto Sans KR (SIL OFL 1.1) for Korean body text.
  • Measured after the changes (automatic bot matches, one seat is a test autopilot). 4 players, five takes: all five reached the 3 minute clock; thieves won 4, constables 1; 4 to 6 catches and 2 to 4 rescues per take. 5 players (1 constable, 4 thieves), two takes with a test autopilot as constable: the constables won one in 100 seconds and the other was cut short. Shoe hits after the auto-aim change: 3 in the last three takes measured.
  • Docs. GDD, itch page, playtest guide, asset list and review record were brought up to date. The itch page now keeps developer-only notes in a separate section at its end.

Not verified yet (as of the end of the night)

  • Play between two PCs over a real network. Everything so far ran on one PC.
  • The audio by ear. All of it, including the new music, was synthesized and nobody has listened to it critically.
  • A trailer video.
  • A packaged build of tonight's version. The last package is from 20:12 and is older than all of the changes above.
  • Balance and fun with real players. The measured bot matches look better (thieves won 4 of 5), but only friends can tell whether the 3 minute take and the bots' rescues feel fair.

Day 3 (오후) — 2026-11-14: 동네 앱 모임의 밤공원 경도로 전환

약 13:40 KST, 제작자가 세계관과 놀이 방식을 바꿨다. 앞선 항목은 당시 버전의 기록으로 그대로 남긴다. 아래 공원 버전은 소스만 작성했고 컴파일·실행·플레이테스트를 하지 않았다. 변경 당시 빌드 PC가 꺼져 있었으며 마지막 검증·패키지는 오늘 새벽의 조선 버전 Shipping 20261114-0016이다.

  • 모임과 캐릭터. 동네 앱에서 만난 평범한 어른들이 밤공원에서 실제 경도를 한다. 형광 러닝복, 머리에 넥타이를 묶은 흰 셔츠 직장인, 버건디 추리닝, 빨간 바람막이와 커다란 보라 햇빛가리개의 등산객을 고른다. 경찰 역할도 회색 후드티·청바지·형광 안전조끼·남색 캡의 참가자다. 제복·배지·계급장은 없다. 기본 마네킹의 신체 영역을 셔츠·바지·피부·신발 색으로 나눠 칠하고 간단한 액세서리를 붙였다.
  • 뼈대 유지. 1인칭, 리슨 서버, 총 3–6명과 빈자리 봇, 혼자 봇과 하는 구조를 유지한다. 3–5명은 경찰 1명, 6명은 2명이다. 20초 숨기 뒤 180초, 역할 교대, 경찰 540cm/s·도둑 480cm/s다. 125cm 안에서 닿아 잡되 벽 너머로 잡지 않는다. 전원 수감이면 경찰 즉시 승리, 시간 종료 때 자유 도둑이 수감자보다 같거나 많으면 도둑 승리다. 여러 도둑이 만든 틈에서 동료를 구한다는 긴장은 같다.
  • 감옥나무와 인간줄. 중앙 느티나무에 첫 수감자가 손을 대고, 다음은 바깥 끝으로 130cm 간격으로 손을 잇는다. 빈 줄은 첫 수감자가 잡힌 방향으로 뻗는다. 잡힌 사람은 움직이지 못하고 감독 모니터에서 클릭으로 경찰 위치를 알린다. 자유 도둑은 초록 띠가 보이는 인간줄 140cm 안에서 E를 유지해 모두 서 있던 자리에서 풀어 준다. 기본 시간은 수감자 1명 1.8초, 2명 1.45초, 3명 1.1초, 4명 이상 0.75초이며 석방 후 3초 보호다. 줄이 길어질수록 바깥 끝에 접근하기 쉽고 더 빨리 끊어진다. 구출 시도 중에는 모두에게 심판 호루라기가 들리는 규칙이다.
  • 문지기 금지. 수감자가 있고 자유 도둑이 나무 11m 안에 없을 때 경찰이 나무 8.5m 안에 7초 머물면 5초 감속된다. 근처 추격은 허용하며 나무 곁에서 버티는 경찰에게 수감자 XP도 주지 않는다.
